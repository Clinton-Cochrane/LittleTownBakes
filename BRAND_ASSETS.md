# Little Town Bakes brand assets

The cookie favicon is included in this repository. The client logo is an owner-supplied launch asset and is not currently checked in.

- Add the transparent logo at `public/brand/little-town-bakes-logo.png`. Use a tightly cropped PNG at least 512 px wide; the header constrains it responsively without distortion and keeps the text brand visible if it cannot load.
- The browser icon at `public/brand/favicon.svg` is a simple cookie inspired by 🍪, with a transparent background and a scalable design for small browser tabs.

These paths are centralized in `lib/brand.ts` if the delivered asset names or formats need to change.
